About

I am a Machine Learning Research Scientist at Apple in Cupertino, where I work on 3D/4D reconstruction and generation. My research sits at the intersection of computer vision, machine learning, and computer graphics, with a long-running focus on understanding 3D humans from images and video.

I received my PhD from ETH Zurich and the Max Planck Institute for Intelligent Systems, advised by Michael J. Black and Otmar Hilliges. Before Apple, I was a Machine Learning Scientist at Meshcapade, and I interned at Apple and NVIDIA. I did my MSc at Middle East Technical University with Emre Akbas and my BSc at Istanbul Technical University.

Research Interests

3D/4D reconstruction and generation · 3D human pose, shape and motion estimation · neural rendering and Gaussian splatting · human–object and human–scene interaction · camera estimation from images and video · generative models for video and 3D · synthetic data
